I am a fourth-year PhD student in Economics at the University of Delaware. My research interests lie at the intersection of labor economics, innovation, and workforce development. My current work examines how technological change, innovation, and workforce-development policies affect employment, skill formation, labor-market outcomes, and economic welfare.

More broadly, I study how public policies, including tax subsidies for data center investment and construction, shape employment, local economic activity, and broader welfare outcomes. My research aims to produce policy-relevant evidence on strategies that promote productivity, expand economic opportunity, and support inclusive workforce development.

I am a fourth-year PhD student in Economics at the University of Delaware. My research spans labor economics, innovation, and the economics of data centers, with a focus on how technological change and public policy shape employment, skill formation, and local economic development. My current work examines how tax subsidies for data center investment and construction affect employment, local economic activity, and economic welfare.

My broader research agenda explores the political economy of data centers, including how political incentives and local institutions shape their location, public support, and the distribution of their costs and benefits. Through this work, I aim to inform policies that promote productivity and expand economic opportunity.
